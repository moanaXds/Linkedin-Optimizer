# AuraProfile

**AuraProfile** is an AI-Powered LinkedIn Profile Analyzer designed to uncover your profile's hidden potential. Upload your LinkedIn profile as a PDF to receive an instant, AI-driven evaluation of your discoverability, experience quality, and recruiter impact.

## Features

- **Keywords & Discoverability**: Analyzes your headline, summary, and experience for term density, relevance, and alignment with target roles, ensuring you surface in the right searches.
- **Metric-Driven Achievements**: Evaluates your listed roles for business impact—quantifiable results, revenue percentages, team sizes, and project scope.
- **Privacy-First**: No signup required and 100% private. 
- **Comprehensive Scoring System**: Get a detailed breakdown of your profile's strength across multiple dimensions:
  - Overall Score (out of 100)
  - Keywords
  - Experience
  - Education
  - Completeness
  - Network
- **Actionable Insights**: Receive tailored recommendations to improve your profile's visibility and impact.
- **Modern UI**: Features a beautiful, glassmorphic design with atmospheric background effects, parallax, and interactive SVG dials.

## How It Works

1. Export your LinkedIn profile as a PDF directly from LinkedIn.
2. Drag and drop the PDF into the AuraProfile upload section.
3. The system parses the PDF structure and analyzes the content against recruiter best practices.
4. Review your personalized scores and actionable recommendations.

## Tech Stack

- **HTML5**: Semantic structure and modern file upload handling.
- **CSS3**: Custom variables, glassmorphism, flexbox/grid layouts, and cinematic animations (aurora, fog, parallax).
- **JavaScript (Vanilla)**: Handles drag-and-drop interactions, file parsing, score calculations, and SVG dial animations.

## Getting Started

To run AuraProfile locally:

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser.
3. Upload a sample LinkedIn PDF to see the analysis in action.
